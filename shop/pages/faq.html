Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping at ARCANE SHOP.

What payment methods do you accept? ▶
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and select cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC). All payments are processed securely with 256-bit SSL encryption.
How long does shipping take? ▶
Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days (free on orders over $75). Express shipping takes 2–3 business days ($9.99). Overnight priority delivers the next business day ($24.99).
What is your return policy? ▶
We offer 30-day free returns on all items. Products must be unused and in original packaging. We cover the return shipping cost. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive the item.
Do you ship internationally? ▶
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries including Canada, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Australia, Japan, and more. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination.
How can I track my order? ▶
Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number on our website or the carrier's site to track your package in real time.
Do you have a loyalty program? ▶
Not yet — but stay tuned! We're building a loyalty program that will reward early subscribers with exclusive discounts, early access to drops, and members-only products.
