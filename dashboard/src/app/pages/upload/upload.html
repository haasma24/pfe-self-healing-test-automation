Expected Format

Paste the raw JSON from your Flask API's /dashboard-data endpoint. It should have these top-level keys:

statsHealingStats — total_attempts, failed_heals, start_time
systemSystemMetrics — ram, cpu, server_status, device
historyArray of past heal calls (can be empty)
lastRunDetailsbestScore, totalCandidates, pipelineTimeSeconds, confidenceGap, layers[], funnel[], candidates[], locators[], stageCounts[], stageTimings[], logs[]

To capture a run from your Colab API:

curl https://<your-url>/dashboard-data | python3 -m json.tool > run.json

This dashboard works with any compatible pipeline — not just Playwright.
